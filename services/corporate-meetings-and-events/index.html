Corporate Meetings & Events

Corporate meetings and conferences, handled end to end by one team

A corporate meeting has to do a job. A leadership offsite has to align people, a sales kick-off has to send them out motivated, an internal congress has to make a strategy land with an audience that has heard a lot of strategy before. We start from that job, not from a venue list.

Elique Events plans and runs corporate meetings and conferences for international organisations, with more than 25 years in the industry behind it. What sets us apart is that it is all one team: concept, venue, delegates, branding, production and the follow-up afterwards. You brief us once instead of coordinating five suppliers who have never met, and there is nobody to point at when something falls between them.

We work across sectors — from life science and healthcare to real estate, sports, entertainment and insurance — and we work as an extension of your team: proactive, contactable, and detailed enough that you can stop worrying about the logistics and concentrate on the content.

What a meeting project covers

Concept and programme design

Working out what the meeting has to achieve, then shaping the agenda, session formats and running order around it.

Venue sourcing and contracting

Finding and negotiating venues that fit the audience, the format and the budget — in the Netherlands and across Europe.

Delegate management

Invitations, registration, travel, accommodation, dietary and accessibility requirements, and the communication that goes with all of it.

Speaker and content coordination

Briefing speakers, collecting and rehearsing content, and keeping presentations on time and on message.

On-site management

A team on the floor running the schedule, the suppliers and the room, so nothing lands on you during the event.

Budget control and reporting

One budget you can follow, with reconciliation afterwards and a debrief on what to change next time.

How we work

  1. Brief

    We work out what the meeting is for, who is coming, and what has to be different by the end of it.

  2. Design

    Concept, format, venue options and a costed plan — enough detail to make a decision on.

  3. Build

    Suppliers, production, delegate communication and rehearsals, tracked against one timeline.

  4. Deliver and review

    We run it on site, then sit down afterwards with what worked and what we would change.

Formats we regularly deliver

  • Sales kick-offs and commercial cycle meetings
  • Internal congresses and town halls
  • Advisory boards and expert meetings
  • Customer and partner conferences
  • Hybrid and broadcast meetings

We work across sectors — HealthCare, LifeScience, Real Estate, Sports, Entertainment and Insurance — and we are equally at home with a format that is not on this list.
